Creamy Fettuccine

This Creamy Fettuccine will serve 4 people and can be cooked in 15-20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 375g Fresh Fettuccina Pasta
  • 20g Butter
  • 175g Shortcut Bacon Rashers, Chopped
  • 2 Garlic Cloves
  • 2 tsp Fresh Rosemary, Finely Chopped
  • 2 Free Range Eggs
  • 2 Egg Yolks
  • 125ml Light Thickened Cooking Cream
  • 82.50ml Parmesan, Finely Grated

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta following packet directions
  2. Meanwhile, melt butter in a frying pan over medium heat. Add bacon. Cook for 4 minutes or until golden. Add garlic and rosemary. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute or until fragrant.
  3. Whisk eggs, yolks, cream and parmesan in a bowl. Season with salt and pepper. Drain pasta and return to saucepan off the heat. Add egg misture and bacon mixture to pasta. Toss for 1 minute or until sauce thickens and coats pasta.
  4. Finally serve and dig in!
